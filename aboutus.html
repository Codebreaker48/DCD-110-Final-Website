About Us

Bluegrass Burnouts is a Youtube Channel that was created by 6 friends who wanted to share their love for NASCAR. Though work and school got in the way, two of those six decided the project would continue. Now, each week of the NASCAR Season, you can join us to hear Zack and Cody talk about the sport that they love.

During the week, we post Race Reviews, Power Rankings, Paint Scheme Previews, and Race Previews. Each one focusing on our own opinions and what we actually thing about the product that NASCAR is producing. You will hear us talk about anything from attendance and horsepower, to rivalries and who is right, and much much more.

Origin

The idea for this channel was birthed as all of the guys involved were beginning to spread out for college. All of the guys met eachother working at Full Throttle Adrenaline Park (FKA Bluegrass Karting) in Louisville Kentucky. In their time there, everyone began to develop a friendship, that naturally entailled their shared love for all motorsports. Each week at work, the races would be on TV and any Sunday shift saw the work raido filled with race updates and conversations over who was going to win. their love for racen extended to the track, as each shift would end with a race between the guys, always striving to be better than one another. Many of the guys involved also spent a lot of time at the races. As many of the guy prepared to leave for college, the idea for a chanel where we talked about the sport we love was born. we had our first ever episode with all 6 members, but unfortunately it just wasnt possible for everyone to meet at the same time each week. Zack and Cody, however, were determined to keep it going.

Whats Next

We are determined to keep the chanel going! Despite college and jobs always getting in the way, we dont want to abandon our little project. We have dreams of it becoming something even bigger than we ever imagined, but even if that never happens, we want to have a way to talk about the sport that we love! if you love NASCAR as much as we do, please join us on our journey and follow us on all of our socials.
